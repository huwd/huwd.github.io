Damn you Rooney,

Stop making me feel like these people are real. Stop making me inhabit their thoughts. Finding some strange recognition with lives that have nothing to do with mine. Weaving me and them into their social fabric, And then bloody ending your books.

I just gatecrashed someone else's life for the weekend, and now they're gone again, I'm back here and it's all just memories.
